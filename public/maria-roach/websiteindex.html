About Maria

Real change doesn't
come from thinking harder.

Hi, I'm Maria. I know what it feels like to carry stress, identity questions, and emotional patterns that don't just magically go away with time — or with talk therapy alone.

Through somatic work and equine-facilitated learning, I've helped individuals and teams reconnect to themselves in ways that feel real, grounded, and sustainable. My approach is body-first: we work with your nervous system rather than against it.

"Horses have been my greatest teachers. They don't perform, they don't judge — they simply respond to what's true in the body. That's the kind of work I bring to every session."

I'm the creator of Mane Discovery, an equine-inspired somatic wellness program that has guided hundreds of women out of survival mode and back into themselves.

Services & Offerings

Each offering is designed to meet you where you are — whether you're just beginning to explore somatic work or deepening an existing practice.

In-Person Equine Sessions

Work alongside horses in a held, therapeutic space. Equine-facilitated sessions offer direct nervous system feedback — horses respond to what's true in your body, creating powerful opportunities for regulation and insight.

Individual Somatic Support

One-on-one sessions for individuals navigating chronic stress, burnout, attachment patterns, or identity questions. We use body-based practices — breath, movement, awareness — to shift what talking alone cannot reach.

My Approach

the way back.

1
Pause & Notice We begin by stepping out of autopilot — noticing what the body is actually doing right now.
2

3
Regulate & Support Using somatic tools — breath, movement, co-regulation — we help your nervous system find safety.
4
Repattern & Integrate New body-based experiences replace old survival patterns, creating lasting, embodied change.
